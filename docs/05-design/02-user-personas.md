# Smart QR Pro

# User Personas

---

# Purpose

This document defines the primary users of Smart QR Pro, their responsibilities, goals, permissions, and common workflows. These personas guide product design and development decisions.

---

# Persona 1 — Super Administrator

## Description

The Super Administrator manages the entire Smart QR Pro platform for the institution.

## Responsibilities

- Manage faculty
- Manage students
- Manage departments
- Manage subjects
- Configure system settings
- View institution-wide reports
- Manage user roles

## Goals

- Maintain accurate student and faculty data
- Ensure the system is configured correctly
- Monitor overall attendance statistics

## Pain Points

- Time-consuming manual administration
- Difficulty tracking attendance across departments
- Generating reports manually

---

# Persona 2 — Faculty

## Description

Faculty members conduct attendance and monitor their assigned classes.

## Responsibilities

- Login securely
- Create attendance sessions
- Generate QR codes
- Monitor live attendance
- View attendance history
- Export reports

## Goals

- Take attendance quickly
- Prevent proxy attendance
- Access attendance reports easily

## Pain Points

- Manual attendance takes time
- Difficulty identifying absent students
- Lack of real-time insights

---

# Persona 3 — Student

## Description

Students use the platform to mark attendance and view their attendance records.

## Responsibilities

- Login
- Scan QR code
- Submit attendance
- View attendance percentage
- View attendance history

## Goals

- Mark attendance easily
- Track attendance percentage
- Stay informed about attendance requirements

## Pain Points

- Forgetting attendance percentage
- Difficulty knowing remaining required attendance
